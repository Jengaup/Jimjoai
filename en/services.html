Services

Process and AI assessment

For businesses where the team works hard, but nobody is quite sure where the time goes or where to start with AI.

I sit down with you and your team and look at how work flows today: who does what, in which tool, and how many times the same thing gets repeated. From there I map where time is lost and what is worth tackling first.

Process mappingPrioritiesAction plan
Let’s talk about this →

What you get

  • A map of your core processes and where they get stuck
  • A list of repetitive tasks that can be automated or supported with AI
  • A prioritized plan: what goes first, what can wait and what isn’t worth it
  • The scope and cost of the implementation, in writing
Real example

At a promotional products business, the information was already in QuickBooks, but nobody could see it. There was no quick way to know which estimates were still waiting for approval or where each package was. Seeing that clearly is what lets you decide what to automate first.

Automation implementation

For teams that repeat the same tasks every day: moving data from one system to another, sending reminders, answering “where’s my order?”

I build the workflows and connect them to the systems you already have. Before I let them run on their own, I test them with your team and walk you through how each part works.

IntegrationsRemindersAutomatic emailsTracking
Let’s talk about this →

What I can build

  • Dashboards connected to systems like QuickBooks, with automatic syncing
  • Automatic emails and reminders to clients, for example to approve estimates
  • A daily email to each person with what’s on their plate that day
  • Calendars of orders and arrivals that update themselves
  • Tracking numbers that get added without anyone copying them
  • Integrations between the tools you already use

I bring your data together in a dashboard that updates itself. At a glance you see what’s pending, what’s on the way and what needs attention today. That’s how you stop reacting and start planning.

05 / 05

AI assistants

For teams that spend a good part of the day reading, sorting and answering messages that look a lot alike.

I design assistants that work inside your process: they prepare a draft, summarize a long thread or sort a request so it reaches the right person. Your team reviews and decides. The assistant gets the work started.

DraftingSummariesSorting
Let’s talk about this →

What they can do

  • Draft replies in your business’s tone of voice
  • Summarize long emails, conversations or documents
  • Sort new requests and send them to the right person
  • Get information ready before your team needs it
How I approach it

I start with a single task, measure it, and only then expand. Anything important always goes through a person before it goes out.
