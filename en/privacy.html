Privacy notice

Your data, in a few words.

This site collects very little information and uses it for one thing only: replying to you. Here I explain what gets collected, where it goes and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Last updated: November 2026

What information I collect

Only what you type into the contact form:

  • Your name and the name of your business or company
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to share it
  • The topics you check and the message you write

Along with your message, the address of the page you wrote from is sent too, so I know where the request came from.

What I use it for

To reply to you and, if you’d like, set up a call. I don’t use it to send you advertising or for anything unrelated to your request.

How it’s sent

The form is sent through Web3Forms, an outside service that receives the message and forwards it to me by email. Web3Forms processes that data under its own privacy policy.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t sell, rent or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • I don’t use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

Third-party services on the site

The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts. When you visit a page, your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them, and Google may receive technical data such as your IP address.

If a booking button takes you to an outside scheduling service, that service has its own privacy policy.

How to ask me to delete your data

Write to me through the contact form and tell me you’d like your information deleted. I’ll delete it and confirm by email. You can also ask me what data I have about you.

Changes to this notice

If I change anything important, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
