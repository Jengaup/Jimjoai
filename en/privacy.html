Privacy notice

Your data, in a nutshell.

This site collects very little information and uses it for one thing: getting back to you. Here’s what is collected, where it goes and how to request access, correction or deletion.

Effective: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

Who is responsible

José Alberto Jiménez, who runs JimjoAI, an independent AI and automation consulting and implementation service, is responsible for this site (ai.josealbertopr.com) and the data it receives. For any privacy matter, use the contact form.

What I collect

Only what you type and send through the contact form:

  • Your name and the name of your business
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to share it
  • The topics you check and the message you write

The address of the page you wrote from is sent along with the message. If you came from the self-assessment, that address includes your type of business and the signs you checked.

Only write what you need to explain your situation. Don’t include passwords, financial data, health information or other people’s personal data.

The self-assessment

The self-assessment on the home page runs entirely in your browser. What you check is not sent or stored. Only if you press the button to contact me are your type of business and signs copied into the form message, and you can edit or delete them before sending.

What I use it for

To reply to you, set up a call if you ask for one, and prevent abusive submissions or spam. I don’t use it to send you advertising or for anything unrelated to your request.

Outside services involved

  • Web3Forms receives the form and forwards it to me by email. It processes that data under its privacy policy.
  • Proton Mail, my email provider, stores the message in my inbox once it arrives. See the Proton privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web server, it may log technical visit data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare manages the domain’s DNS. When its proxy service is active, it also processes visits, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Calendly is only involved if you press a button to book a call. At that point you leave this site and Calendly handles your data under its privacy notice.

Fonts are served from this site itself, with no connections to Google Fonts.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses no cookies, advertising or analytics. After you send the form, it saves only the time of sending in your browser (sessionStorage) to prevent duplicate messages for one minute. That value contains no personal information, never leaves your browser and is deleted when you close the tab.

How long it’s kept

I keep messages sent through the form for 60 days from the day I receive them, then delete them from my inbox. If you’d like yours deleted sooner, ask me as explained below.

Access, correction or deletion of your data

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it or ask me to delete it. To do so:

  1. Open the data request. The form opens with the message already drafted.
  2. Use the same email you wrote to me from, so I can find your message.
  3. Say whether you want access, correction or deletion and, if you remember it, the approximate date of your message.

You don’t need to send documents or any additional personal data. I’ll reply by email.

Minors

This site is aimed at businesses and isn’t meant for minors to submit information.

Security

The site runs over HTTPS, limits what the browser can load and uses measures against automated submissions. No system on the internet is completely secure, so I can’t guarantee absolute security.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t sell, rent or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • I don’t use tracking cookies, pixels, advertising or analytics on this site.

Changes to this notice

If I change anything important, I’ll update this page and the date above.
