Case 01 · Customized promotional products

From putting out fires to seeing every order at a glance

A business that sells customized promotional products was doing almost everything by hand. QuickBooks was only used to prepare estimates and purchase orders (POs).

The challenge

The information existed, but nobody could see the full picture. There was no visibility into orders, which estimates were still waiting for approval or where each package was.

Answering any of those questions meant digging by hand. The business spent its days reacting to whatever came up.

What I did

  • A dashboard that pulls in all the QuickBooks information through automatic syncs
  • Automatic emails that remind clients to approve their estimate
  • A daily email to each salesperson with what’s pending for them that day
  • A calendar of incoming orders and arrivals
  • Tracking numbers that get added on their own

The result

25% of their time back. Hours that used to go into searching, confirming and chasing.

Clients came out ahead too: an automatic system lets them know where their order is, without anyone having to write to them.

Before and now

Before

Pending orders and estimates weren’t visible anywhere.

Now

A dashboard synced with QuickBooks shows everything in one place.

Before

Getting a client to approve an estimate depended on someone remembering to email them.

Now

The approval reminder goes out on its own.

Before

Knowing what each salesperson had to do that day meant checking by hand.

Now

Each salesperson gets an email with their pending tasks for the day.

Before

Finding a package meant hunting down the tracking number.

Now

Tracking is added automatically, and the client knows where their order is.

Case 02 · Sales department

Less time chasing clients, more time finding new ones

I worked with a company’s entire sales department. The job had two parts: teach the team to use AI in their work, and automate client follow-up.

The challenge

Client follow-up took up a big chunk of every rep’s week. It’s necessary work, but repetitive: emails, reminders and keeping track of who still hasn’t replied.

Every hour spent there was one less hour finding new clients.

What I did

  • Trained the whole department, not just a few people
  • Taught them to use AI on the real, everyday tasks of sales
  • We automated the client follow-up process
  • Everything designed so the team can run it on their own

The result

18% of follow-up time, back.

The reps got back almost a fifth of the time they spent on follow-up. Today they use that time to find new clients.

Before and now

Before

Follow-up was done by hand, one client at a time.

Now

Client follow-up is automated.

Before

Follow-up time competed with finding new clients.

Now

That recovered 18% now goes into prospecting.
